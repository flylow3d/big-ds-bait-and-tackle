The lakes guide

101 lakes. One bait stop.

Steuben County is Indiana's lake country — and Big D's sits right on the corridor between Angola and the big glacial chain at Pokagon State Park.

Pick your water

Where's it biting?

A few favorites from the 101 — swing by the shop and ask what's hot this week; the advice is free with the minnows.

Lake James

~1,200 acres

Indiana's third-largest natural lake — a deep, clear glacial chain wrapped around Pokagon State Park, with two public beaches.

SmallmouthWalleyePerchPanfish

Crooked Lake

802 acres

A hard-water favorite come winter — and the DNR stocked walleye fingerlings here in fall 2025, so the class of '25 is coming of age.

WalleyeBluegillBassIce fishing

Clear Lake

One of Indiana's clearest

Lives up to the name — gin-clear water and a rare northeast-Indiana shot at rainbow trout, plus 2025-stocked walleye.

WalleyeNorthern pikeRainbow trout

Hamilton Lake

~1,000 acres

The county's second-biggest water — room to run and plenty of shoreline to work.

BassCrappieBluegill

Pleasant Lake

"Nipcondish"

Originally named Nipcondish — "gentle waters" — and still exactly that. A quiet bluegill-and-largemouth kind of morning.

BluegillLargemouth

Jimmerson · Snow · the Pigeon chain

The James chain

The connected waters around Lake James — hop lake to lake by boat and follow the bite.
